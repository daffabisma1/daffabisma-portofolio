A LITTLE INTRODUCTION

Turning field data into spatial understanding.

I have professional experience in geodetic surveying, geospatial mapping, and water resources consulting. Since May 2024, I have been working as a Technical Staff/GIS at PT Aditya Engineering Consultant in Bandung.

My work covers topographic surveys, bathymetry, hydro-oceanographic surveys, GNSS, UAV mapping, orthophoto and DEM processing, as well as raster and vector-based spatial data analysis.

Through various planning and technical investigation projects, I have been involved in watershed analysis, water demand assessment, flood mapping, water infrastructure inventory, and technical documentation.
